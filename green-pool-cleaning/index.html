Why did my pool turn green?

Low or ineffective sanitizer, organic debris, interrupted circulation, and changing water balance can combine. A pool can also take on a green tint from metals, so color alone does not establish algae. Tell the team whether walls are slippery, the water is cloudy, and the pump has stopped recently. one-time cleaning guide explains why recovery starts with the system as well as the water.

Will adding more shock clear the pool?

Not necessarily. A treatment cannot remove a large debris load or repair a circulation fault. Recent chemical additions also matter when interpreting the next water test. Share product names and when they were added rather than layering on more treatments while guessing at the cause. The cleanup scope should address testing, removal, circulation, and filtration together.

Does a green pool need draining?

A green appearance alone is not a reason to empty a pool. Construction, groundwater, weather, water condition, and the proposed treatment all affect the decision. Leave the water in place until the team assesses the approach. A cleanup is different from an acid wash or surface treatment, and neither should be assumed from a photograph alone.

Can cleanup be finished in one visit?

Some pools need follow-up attention as suspended material clears and filters collect debris. Others first need a pump or filter fault addressed. Ask what the initial visit includes, what progress will be checked, and what extra visits or filter service could be needed. A fixed recovery time without seeing the pool would hide those differences.

Why did it turn green after rain?

Storms can add debris and runoff while changing the load on sanitizer and filtration. Explain what entered the pool, whether the water overflowed, and whether equipment lost power. green pool recovery guide describes why the response should follow the cause rather than a standard chemical recipe.

When can the pool be used again?

Confirm the return-to-use instructions after cleanup and testing. Clear-looking water alone is not proof that water care is complete. Keep swimmers out when the water is green or unusually cloudy, or when the bottom cannot be seen clearly. Ask who checks the follow-up readings and what conditions must be met before the pool returns to normal use.

How much does green pool cleanup cost?

Debris volume, water condition, equipment operation, pool size, access, treatment needs, and follow-up work affect the quote. Confirm the initial scope, any filter service, and the approval process if further work is needed. Ask how ongoing weekly maintenance can help address the conditions that allowed the problem to develop.

What should I send before booking?

Photos, equipment labels, recent service history, and the actual property address help the team establish the starting condition. Tell the team about a preferred date, gate access, or pets when requesting a visit. Confirm scheduling, the initial scope, and pricing directly.
