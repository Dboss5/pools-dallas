REAL QUESTIONS FROM POOL OWNERS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Can I book regular pool cleaning?

Yes. PoolBurg offers weekly pool service. Contact the team with your location, pool size, and current condition to discuss the scope, schedule, and price for your property.

My pool is losing water. Which service should I choose?

PoolBurg offers pool leak detection and repair. Describe the water loss, when you first noticed it, and any visible wet areas so the team can discuss an inspection.

Do you repair pool pumps, filters, and heaters?

PoolBurg lists pool pump, filter, heater, plumbing, and electrical repair services. Share the equipment brand, model, and symptoms to confirm the appropriate service.

How much does pool service in Dallas cost?

Request a quote for your pool. Maintenance and repair scope varies with the pool, its condition, the equipment, and the work required. Confirm the service inclusions and any assessment charges with the team.

Can you help with a pool remodel or worn finish?

PoolBurg offers pool remodeling, resurfacing, tile replacement, and acid washing services. The suitable approach depends on the existing finish and its condition; discuss an assessment before selecting the work.

Does the service guide book an appointment?

No. The guide creates a brief you can save. Use the Request service link or call 469-277-9893 to contact PoolBurg and confirm an appointment.
