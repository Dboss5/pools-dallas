Where do I start with a neglected pool?

Start by documenting the water, visible surfaces, and equipment rather than assuming the pool needs a full renovation. For a Fort Worth property that has been vacant or has an unknown maintenance history, ask which initial cleanup, equipment checks, and follow-up care belong in the first scope. A neglected pool and a maintained pool should not receive the same sight-unseen plan.

Should I repair the pool before remodeling?

Investigate recurring water loss, plumbing concerns, and equipment problems before new cosmetic work covers them. If the same area will need access later, coordinating the repair now can avoid disturbing a finished surface. pool remodeling guide lays out why hidden work belongs early in the plan.

Can I keep my existing pool equipment?

Ask for an assessment of condition and compatibility before replacing the whole pad. Serviceable equipment may be worth retaining, while a failing component or unsupported control may change the renovation scope. For an older Fort Worth pool, photographs and model numbers are more useful than age estimates alone.

What does pool restoration actually include?

Restoration may mean water recovery and targeted repairs, or a larger scope involving finishes, equipment, and surrounding details. The proposal should state what is being restored and what is excluded. pool remodeling guide explains why two projects described as restoration can contain very different work.

How much does pool service cost in Fort Worth?

Start with the work your pool needs: recurring care, an initial cleanup, a diagnostic visit, or a renovation. Pool size, condition, equipment, access, and the agreed scope affect the quote. Share any inspection report, previous repair paperwork, and whether the pool is currently operating. Include your priorities in order: dependable circulation, water recovery, surface condition, or appearance. Ask what is included, what is charged separately, and how additional work is approved before comparing proposals.

Can I book a visit to my Fort Worth property?

Send the property address, a short description of the problem, and photos through Frisco Pool Servicess’s service request or call the team. You can discuss one issue or several connected concerns in the same request. Confirm the appointment, assessment scope, access arrangements, and any diagnostic fee directly with the team.
