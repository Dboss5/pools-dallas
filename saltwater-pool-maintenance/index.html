Does a saltwater pool still use chlorine?

Yes. A salt chlorine generator uses the pool’s saltwater to produce chlorine. It does not remove leaves or replace brushing, filtration, and water testing. The maintenance conversation should cover the whole pool, not just the chlorinator. Tell the team the model and whether the water or display has changed.

Why is my saltwater pool turning green?

Chlorine production and the pool’s current sanitizer demand may not be keeping up, but the cause needs investigation. Flow, settings, operating conditions, scale, and equipment faults can all matter. A green pool may need a recovery assessment alongside checking the salt system, rather than simply adding salt or replacing the cell.

Does a check-cell light mean replacement?

Not automatically. The meaning depends on the model and exact warning, including whether it is a reminder or an operating fault. Record the display and model before resetting or buying parts. saltwater maintenance guide explains why inspection, salt measurement, and flow may need attention before a replacement decision.

How often should a salt cell be cleaned?

Follow the model’s maintenance instructions and the inspection findings. Water balance, scale, use, and conditions affect the need; unnecessary chemical cleaning should not become a routine substitute for diagnosis. saltwater maintenance guide explains why one fixed calendar does not suit every system.

Is cell cleaning included in weekly care?

Weekly care includes salt-system attention, but confirm how the selected plan handles routine inspection, cleaning, and more involved work. An acid-cleaning task, failed sensor, or replacement cell can be a different scope from the weekly visit. Ask what the visit notes will record and how additional work will be approved.

Why does the system read low salt after cleaning?

The displayed value needs to be considered alongside the actual water, system model, temperature, flow, and equipment condition. Adding salt solely because of a warning can create another problem if the reading is inaccurate. Share recent additions and changes so the assessment can establish what is happening before further adjustment.

What affects saltwater maintenance pricing?

Pool size, current condition, access, debris load, cell-care needs, and the agreed cleaning scope affect the plan. Confirm standard chemical and service inclusions, with separate pricing for specialty treatments, diagnosis, or parts. Compare it with weekly maintenance using the same scope rather than assuming salt equipment eliminates routine work.

What should I send before booking?

Photos, equipment labels, recent service history, and the actual property address help the team establish the starting condition. Tell the team about a preferred date, gate access, or pets when requesting a visit. Confirm scheduling, the initial scope, and pricing directly.
