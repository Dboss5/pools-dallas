START WITH THE CONDITION OF YOUR POOL

Where should I start?

Acid washing is a surface treatment, not a repair for every discolored pool. The finish type, remaining condition, previous treatments, and source of staining all matter. A worn or failing finish may need renovation instead of another aggressive cleaning.

Will an acid wash remove pool stains?

It can improve some staining on suitable pool finishes, but the result depends on the stain and the surface. Organic marks, metal staining, scale, and damage beneath a finish do not all respond to the same approach. Ask for an assessment of the stain type, treatment options, and likely limitations before agreeing to drain or chemically treat the pool.

Why do rust stains keep coming back?

Rust-colored marks can relate to fill water, metal debris, fixtures, or an underlying source beneath the finish. Cleaning the visible stain alone may leave that source in place. surface and stain care guide explains why the pattern and location matter when deciding whether cleaning or further repair is appropriate.

Do I need acid washing or resurfacing?

The distinction is the condition of the finish. A sound but stained surface may be a cleaning candidate. Areas that are failing, worn through, or separating require a different assessment. Surface cleaning does not rebuild missing material or repair structural movement. If the finish is deteriorating, compare a pool renovation scope before paying for a cosmetic treatment.

Does a green pool need acid washing?

Not automatically. Green water can involve sanitation, circulation, debris, or metals; a green color does not by itself establish that the finish needs acid washing. Start with water and equipment assessment. green pool recovery guide shows why the cause should be identified before choosing the treatment.

Will the pool need to be drained?

Some surface work involves draining, but that decision depends on the treatment, pool construction, weather, groundwater, and site conditions. Have the team determine the method and timing. Do not empty the pool in preparation for a quote. Ask who handles draining, discharge arrangements, refilling, and water balancing if those steps are part of the work.

How much does a pool acid wash cost?

Pool size, staining, finish condition, access, preparation, and the agreed drain-and-refill work affect the quote. Confirm what happens if the assessment shows that the finish is too worn for the proposed treatment. Ask whether water, refill time, startup care, or repair work are separate responsibilities.
