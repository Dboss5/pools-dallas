THE WORK BEHIND THE RESULT

Check the result

Discuss post-service pressure and circulation under comparable operating conditions and any parts needing further attention.

How do I know my filter needs cleaning?

Use the manufacturer’s guidance for your model and compare the current reading with the clean baseline at a similar pump setting. A pressure change means more when the operating conditions are comparable. Share the date of the last service, the gauge reading, and whether flow changed. There is no single pressure number that diagnoses every filter.

Why is pressure high after cleaning?

The element may still be loaded, a component may be damaged, or a restriction elsewhere may remain. A faulty gauge or a different pump speed can also confuse the comparison. Repeated cleaning without a useful improvement is a reason to assess the system rather than simply repeat the same task. Equipment diagnosis may be a separate part of the work.

Do cartridge filters need backwashing?

Cartridge and sand or DE filters use different maintenance methods. Cartridge filters require care for their elements rather than treating them like a backwashable sand system. Tell the team the filter model before choosing a service. Follow that model’s instructions; never open a filter while it is pressurized. filter cleaning guide explains what happens when the element itself has reached the end of useful service.

Does cleaning fix a damaged cartridge?

No. Torn media, damaged end caps, broken supports, or deformed elements may allow debris to bypass filtration even after cleaning. The assessment should distinguish dirt that can be removed from material that needs replacement. Ask whether replacement elements are included in the estimate or priced separately, and confirm the exact compatible part.

Is filter deep cleaning part of weekly service?

Confirm the plan’s boundaries. Routine observations and model-appropriate filter attention can form part of care, while disassembly, deep cleaning, replacement media, or a repair may be separate. weekly pool care guide is useful when comparing a regular visit with a dedicated filter appointment.

Why does the filter clog again so quickly?

A pool recovering from algae or carrying a heavy debris load can load its filter quickly. Cleaning also cannot eliminate a continuing source of fine material or correct every circulation issue. Tell the team if the water has been green, construction or landscaping occurred nearby, or the same symptoms returned immediately after service.

What affects the filter-cleaning quote?

Filter type, size, access, internal condition, cleaning scope, and any replacement elements or seals affect the work. Ask whether the quote includes inspection, cleaning, reassembly, and an operating check. If the tank, clamp, or other pressure-bearing component is leaking, describe it before arranging normal service.

What should I send before booking?

Photos, equipment labels, recent service history, and the actual property address help the team establish the starting condition. Tell the team about a preferred date, gate access, or pets when requesting a visit. Confirm scheduling, the initial scope, and pricing directly.
