PLAN BEFORE SWITCH-ON

A clear path to a McKinney display.

01 / Gather the views

Photograph the front, approach, and any side elevation you want lit. Include planting and outdoor outlets in your notes. A dusk photo can reveal where existing lighting already does useful work.

02 / Confirm the constraints

Share roof access, gates, irrigation, operating hours, and any owner or association requirements. Let the installation team assess mounting, electrical supply, and safe placement at the actual property.

03 / Agree the whole season

Ask for a written scope, preferred ready-by date, and the handling of care, removal, and storage. A visual concept helps communicate the look; the quote defines the work.

BEFORE THE FIRST TWINKLE

McKinney Christmas light
installation questions.

How do I request a Christmas lighting quote in McKinney?

Call 945-777-3345 with your McKinney property location, photos, preferred ready-by date, and the features you want lit. The team will confirm the proposed scope, access needs, scheduling, and property-specific pricing. The online preview does not reserve a date.

What should I check before approving the display?

Detailed trim can disappear behind an oversized display. Keep the first concept restrained and add layers only where they improve the view. Confirm any applicable owner or historic-property requirements. Ask for the agreed features, material ownership, installation approach, and removal arrangements in writing.

Can I preview my own home before deciding?

Yes. The house visualizer lets you add a photo, mark rooflines or other areas, and choose color and fixture styles. Adjust size, quantity, brightness, and glow, then compare day and simulated night views. It is an illustrative design tool, not an engineering or electrical assessment.

Are removal and storage included?

Confirm them in the quote. Installation, seasonal care, removal, storage, replacement parts, and next-year setup may be separate items. Ask who owns the equipment and how labeled sections will be kept for another season.

Do you have a separate office in McKinney?

This page describes a service area, not a city-specific office. Call to confirm the property location and current installation availability. There is no separate local address or guaranteed response time represented on this page.
