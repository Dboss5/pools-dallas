Why does my spa heat but my pool stays cold?

A pool and spa have different water volumes and may use different operating modes, valve positions, and temperature settings. Describe what each mode does and whether the behavior changed recently. The heater may be producing heat while the control or plumbing configuration prevents the expected result in the pool.

Why does my heater stop before reaching temperature?

A schedule, sensor reading, flow condition, or fault can interrupt operation. Note the exact display message and how long the heater runs before stopping. PoolBurg’s electric-heater diagnosis article explains why the model and operating conditions come before replacing a heating component.

Can heating and cooling use the same equipment?

Some reversible heat pumps support both, while other systems only heat or require separate cooling equipment. Confirm the actual model capabilities and how the unit connects to automation. PoolBurg’s heat-pump troubleshooting guide is useful when a unit runs but the result falls short of expectations.

Can I upgrade comfort during a remodel?

Yes, discuss comfort goals while the renovation scope is still being planned. Equipment footprint, utilities, controls, and plumbing can affect later finish or deck work. Ask which existing components can stay and what work is required to support the proposed heater, cooler, or control upgrade.

How much does pool service cost in Southlake?

Start with the work your pool needs: recurring care, an initial cleanup, a diagnostic visit, or a renovation. Pool size, condition, equipment, access, and the agreed scope affect the quote. Share whether the issue affects the pool, spa, or both; the desired temperature; and photos of the controller and equipment. For a Southlake renovation, include planned changes around the equipment area. Ask what is included, what is charged separately, and how additional work is approved before comparing proposals.

Can I book a visit to my Southlake property?

Send the property address, a short description of the problem, and photos through PoolBurg’s service request or call the team.
