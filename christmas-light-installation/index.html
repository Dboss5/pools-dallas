SERVICE & INSTALLATION PLANNING

Christmas roofline and entrance lighting

A Christmas installation can begin with the front roofline and grow into a coordinated display across the entrance and landscape. Identify the roof peaks and elevations that matter most from the street, then decide which areas should remain quieter. Bring front and side photos so the installation discussion covers the actual building rather than a generic package.

PLAN YOUR LIGHTING INSTALLATION

01. Start with the roofline

A continuous outline gives the whole property a clear shape after dark. Bring photos showing the front elevation, roof peaks, and any side elevations visible from the street.

02. Give the entrance a moment

A doorway, porch, or front-facing window can become the focal point. Decide which feature should lead, then keep nearby details quieter so the display feels intentional.

03. Choose one visual rhythm

Warm white creates a gentle glow; red and white makes the Christmas theme more pronounced. Repeating one palette across roofline and landscape helps different features belong together.

GOOD TO KNOW

Christmas Light Installation questions.

Can I start with just the roofline?

A roofline is a useful starting concept. Ask the team to quote that scope separately from optional entrance and landscape features.

Do you need photos before quoting?

Photos help communicate scale and style, but they do not replace checking access, measurements, electrical supply, and the final installation scope.

What affects a roofline installation quote?

Discuss the measured roofline, building height, roof access, fixture style, attachment requirements, and preferred installation date. Ask for optional entrance and landscape features to be listed separately so you can prioritize the display.
