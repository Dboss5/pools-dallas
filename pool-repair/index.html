Why won’t my pool pump start?

Possible causes include the power supply, controls, a motor problem, or a drive fault. A schedule or automation setting can also make a working pump appear unresponsive. Note the exact model, any displayed message, and whether the problem began after a power outage or another equipment change. Repeated breaker trips need qualified diagnosis rather than repeated resets.

Why is my pump running without flow?

A running motor does not prove that water is circulating properly. Low pool water, a suction-side air leak, restricted baskets, valve position, or an internal obstruction can affect prime and flow. If the pump is running dry, turn it off and request service. Photograph the equipment configuration rather than opening the motor or reaching into a running pump.

Does a leaking pump need replacing?

Not always. Water can escape at a lid seal, union, drain plug, shaft seal, or cracked housing. The repair decision depends on the actual leak point, motor condition, parts availability, and system compatibility. pool equipment repair guide explains why a puddle beside the pump is not a complete diagnosis.

Why does my filter pressure keep rising?

A filter can load with debris during normal cleaning, but rapid repeat pressure changes can signal an ongoing water problem, cleaning issue, or restriction. Compare readings under similar operating conditions and report the clean baseline if you have it. Do not open a pressurized filter. A leaking tank or clamp needs service before normal operation resumes.

Should I repair or replace the equipment?

Compare the repair scope with the condition and compatibility of the whole system. A localized repair can make sense when the remaining equipment is sound. Replacement may be more practical when several components are failing or parts are unavailable. pool equipment repair guide shows why a quote should identify equipment, installation, controls, and startup separately.

How much does pool equipment repair cost?

The failed component, diagnostic work, access, replacement parts, and any plumbing or control changes affect the quote. Ask what the assessment includes, whether the repair is separate, and what post-repair checks are planned. Share warranty documents if you have them; coverage must be confirmed for the specific equipment and fault.
