Is my pool equipment still under warranty?

Coverage depends on the product, installation, dates, terms, and available records. A manufacturer name alone does not establish eligibility. Locate the model and serial label, invoice, and installer information. Ask the responsible warranty provider what documentation is needed and whether the issue should be assessed through a particular service process.

Are you an authorized warranty provider?

Call with the manufacturer, model, and warranty details to confirm whether your request can be handled through the required authorized service process. Manufacturers may require a particular provider or prior approval. Establish those arrangements before scheduling a repair, and use the manufacturer’s service process where an authorized provider is required.

Does warranty cover diagnosis and labor?

Do not assume that parts coverage means every visit, diagnosis, removal, installation, or related repair is included. Ask the responsible provider which costs are covered, which need approval, and what happens if the fault is not eligible. Keep the warranty decision separate from an ordinary chargeable service quote.

Should I replace the failed part first?

Before authorizing work that may affect a claim, confirm the required process and documentation with the warranty provider. Record the symptom and keep relevant service history. An unapproved replacement can complicate the next step. Immediate hazards should be handled promptly by the appropriate professional rather than waiting for a routine claims discussion.

What if the claim is not accepted?

Ask for the reason and the applicable terms from the responsible provider. If chargeable diagnosis or repair is proposed, request a separate written scope and price before agreeing. A declined claim does not itself identify the most suitable repair or prove that complete equipment replacement is necessary.

What should I send with an inquiry?

Provide the model, serial number, installation date if known, invoice or installer details, exact error, and a short history of the issue. Keep personal account details and access codes private. Confirm who will communicate with the manufacturer and whether an appointment can proceed before authorization is complete.
