A good first line: tap one end of a roof edge, tap the peak, then tap the other end. Lights follow the connected points. Choose Finish line before moving to another roof section or a tree.

Keyboard: focus the photo, use arrows to move the cursor (Shift for larger steps), Enter to add a point, Escape to finish, Backspace to undo. Photos and designs are cleared when you leave or reload this page.
