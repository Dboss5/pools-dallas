ABOUT THE PHOTO CHECK

What happens to my pictures?

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No photo bytes are sent by this checker. Photos are processed in this browser tab. The face-check software and model are served with this site and run on your device. Accepted pictures are kept as pixels in temporary memory, with no original filename or photo metadata retained by the tool. Clear them with the button or leave the page to discard them.

What if a picture contains a face?

A detected face causes the photo to be rejected before a preview or water-colour analysis is added. The checker also refuses the photo if the face scan cannot complete. Face detection is imperfect, especially for small, obscured, or side-on faces; only choose photos without people.

Can this tell me the exact leak location?

No. Your answers can point to useful inspection areas, but a photograph cannot locate a hidden plumbing leak or test equipment. The leak detection guide explains the assessment and testing questions to discuss.

Why do I need to mark the water?

Grass, trees, deck, shadows, and reflections can share colours with a pool problem. Marking only the water makes the colour observation more relevant. A colour pattern is still only a visible clue, not proof of algae or a water-chemistry result.

Can I use the guide without a photo?

Yes. Select the symptom and answer the questions. The result will say that no photo evidence was used and suggest relevant inspection areas and service guides from your answers.
