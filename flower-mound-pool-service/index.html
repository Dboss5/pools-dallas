Why do pool stains return after cleaning?

The treatment may remove a visible mark without removing its source. The pattern can relate to water chemistry, metal debris, fixtures, or an issue beneath the finish. Photograph the mark when it returns and note any refill or equipment changes. PoolBurg’s recurring-stain guide explains why the location matters.

Does my pool need an acid wash?

A sound but stained finish may be a candidate, but the surface type and condition must be checked first. A rough, worn-through, or separating finish needs a different assessment. For a Flower Mound pool, ask which problem the proposed treatment addresses and what result it cannot provide before approving draining or chemical treatment.

Can a green pool be cleaned without resurfacing?

Green water does not automatically mean the finish has failed. Water testing, debris assessment, and circulation checks help establish the initial work. PoolBurg’s guide to green water after rain explains why color alone should not decide between cleanup, acid washing, and renovation.

Should I resurface or remodel the pool?

Resurfacing addresses the interior finish; remodeling can also include tile, coping, equipment, and surrounding details. Start with the areas that are actually damaged and the changes you want. Discuss hidden repairs before choosing finish materials, especially if the pool also loses water or has recurring equipment problems.

How much does pool service cost in Flower Mound?

Start with the work your pool needs: recurring care, an initial cleanup, a diagnostic visit, or a renovation. Pool size, condition, equipment, access, and the agreed scope affect the quote. Send close-ups of affected steps, walls, tile, or floor plus a wide pool photo. Include the finish’s approximate age, previous treatments, and whether marks return in the same location. Ask what is included, what is charged separately, and how additional work is approved before comparing proposals.

Can I book a visit to my Flower Mound property?

Send the property address, a short description of the problem, and photos through PoolBurg’s service request or call the team. You can discuss one issue or several connected concerns in the same request. Confirm the appointment, assessment scope, access arrangements, and any diagnostic fee directly with the team.
